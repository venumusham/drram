Sebaceous cyst removal is a small procedure, but technique matters. The aim is not just to empty the cyst, but to remove the complete cyst sac while keeping the incision as small and cosmetic as possible.

In this Short, Dr. M. Ram Prabhu shows the concept of micro-surgical cyst removal for minimal scarring.

## Why cysts come back

If only the contents of a sebaceous cyst are squeezed out, the sac may remain inside. The cyst can refill and recur. Permanent treatment usually requires complete removal of the sac.

## Why scar planning matters

Cysts on visible areas need careful incision placement, gentle tissue handling, and precise closure. A plastic surgery approach helps reduce unnecessary scarring.

## Key takeaway

For cysts that are growing, recurring, painful, or cosmetically bothersome, planned excision is better than repeated squeezing or drainage at home.
