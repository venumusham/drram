# Review Request Templates — English / Telugu / Hindi

**Goal:** 140 → 200+ Google reviews in 90 days (target: +30 reviews/month)

**Why this matters:**
- Each review = ranking signal in Google local pack
- Star rating in SERP increases CTR by 25-35%
- Quoted reviews can be reused on website testimonials
- Negative review prevention: ask happy patients first

---

## ⏱️ Timing Strategy — When to Send

| Procedure | First message | Follow-up (if no response) | Stop after |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day-care (lipoma, earlobe, small lipo) | 24 hours after | 7 days after | 14 days |
| Major surgery (gynecomastia, rhino, lipo) | 7-10 days after (post first follow-up) | 21 days after | 35 days |
| Mommy makeover, facelift, tummy tuck | 14-21 days after | 35 days after | 50 days |
| International / NRI patients | After remote follow-up at 3 weeks | 6 weeks | 8 weeks |

**Rule:** Send 1 reminder maximum. After 2 messages without response, stop. Never push.

---

## 🎯 Direct Link Setup (Do This Once)

Get your direct Google review link:
1. Open your GBP at `business.google.com`
2. Click "Get more reviews" → "Share review form"
3. Copy the short URL (looks like `g.page/r/XXXXX/review`)
4. Replace `[REVIEW_LINK]` in all templates below with this URL

**Example:** `https://g.page/r/CdBvX_Me5RKfEAg/review`

Also generate a QR code (free at qr-code-generator.com) → print on:
- Discharge papers
- Business cards
- Reception desk display

## ⚠️ What NOT to Do

| Don't | Why |
|---|---|
| ❌ Ask for "5-star reviews" | Google penalises review gating; some patients give honest 4-star reviews and that's fine |
| ❌ Offer discounts/incentives for reviews | Violates Google guidelines; can cause review removal or account suspension |
| ❌ Ask multiple times after no response | Annoys patients, can backfire to negative review |
| ❌ Send from random numbers | Use the registered clinic WhatsApp Business number for consistency |
| ❌ Reply to bad reviews defensively | Always thank for feedback, offer to discuss privately, keep professional |
| ❌ Write reviews from staff accounts | Google detects this; permanent ban risk |
| ❌ Bulk-send identical messages from same number | Triggers WhatsApp spam protection |

---

## ✅ Best Practices

| Do | Why |
|---|---|
| ✅ Personalise with patient name | 3x higher response rate than generic |
| ✅ Send during business hours (10AM-7PM) | Better open rates |
| ✅ Use the patient's preferred language | More likely they engage |
| ✅ Make the link the first thing they see | Reduce friction to single tap |
| ✅ Use WhatsApp Business with quick-reply templates | Consistency + speed |
| ✅ Track in a simple spreadsheet | Patient name, procedure, date sent, response |
| ✅ Respond to every review within 24h | Algorithm signal + reputation |
| ✅ Encourage detail (procedure, recovery) | Helps SEO & future patients |
