Some insect or bug-related skin injuries can look like ordinary skin infections. If the cause is misunderstood, treatment may be delayed or misdirected.

This Short discusses how certain skin injuries can be confused with simple infection and why proper assessment matters.

## Why diagnosis matters

Redness, swelling, pain, discharge, and skin breakdown can occur in many conditions. Not every red painful skin lesion is a routine infection. Some injuries may involve local tissue reaction, necrosis, allergy, or secondary infection.

## When to get checked

Medical evaluation is important when pain is increasing, the wound is spreading, fever develops, pus appears, skin turns dark, or the wound does not improve with basic care.

## Key takeaway

Skin wounds that look unusual, spread quickly, or fail to heal should be evaluated early. Correct diagnosis helps avoid delayed wound care and poor healing.
