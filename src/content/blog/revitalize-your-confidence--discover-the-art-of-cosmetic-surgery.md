## When cosmetic surgery may be appropriate

You might be a good candidate if:

- You are in stable general health and at a steady weight (for body procedures)
- You understand that surgery refines contour; it does not replace lifestyle
- You have a specific concern (asymmetry, excess skin, disproportion) rather than vague dissatisfaction
- You can follow recovery restrictions for the recommended timeline

Cosmetic surgery is a poor fit if you expect perfection, are pressured by others, or hope surgery will fix unrelated stress or self-esteem without counselling when needed.

## What a proper consultation includes

A thorough consultation should cover:

1. **Your goals** — what bothers you, and what “success” looks like in clothing and daily life  
2. **Alternatives** — non-surgical options, waiting, or a different procedure  
3. **Technique and scars** — incision placement, likely scar length, and visibility over time  
4. **Risks** — infection, bleeding, asymmetry, sensation change, anaesthesia considerations  
5. **Recovery** — time off work, garment use, exercise limits, follow-up schedule  
6. **Costs** — all-inclusive package vs itemised add-ons (see our [services](/services) pages for procedure-specific detail)

## Choosing a plastic surgeon in Hyderabad

Look for **DNB or MCh Plastic Surgery**, active medical council registration, hospital privileges, and experience in the procedure you want — not just “cosmetic doctor” branding.

Ask to see healed results in patients with a similar starting point (with consent). Read reviews for patterns (communication, follow-up, complication handling), not only star ratings.
