Gynecomastia myths can delay treatment for years. Many men are told that chest gland enlargement is only fat, that it will always disappear with gym workouts, or that they should simply ignore it.

This Short addresses common myths around gynecomastia.

## Common myths

One myth is that all male chest fullness is fat. In reality, many patients have firm gland tissue behind the nipple. Another myth is that exercise can remove gland. Exercise builds muscle, but it does not remove established gland tissue.

## Why myths matter

Believing myths can cause delay, embarrassment, and years of hiding. A consultation helps separate fat, gland, skin excess, and posture-related concerns.

## Key takeaway

Gynecomastia is a medical and anatomical condition, not a character flaw. Correct information helps patients make calm, informed choices.
