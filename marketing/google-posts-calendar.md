# Google Posts 90-Day Content Calendar — Dr. Ramprabhu Plastic Surgery Clinic

**Cadence:** 2 posts per week (Tue + Fri) for 13 weeks = 26 posts
**Where:** Google Business Profile (GBP) → "Add update" or "Add offer"
**Why:** Active GBPs rank higher in local pack. Each post stays prominent for 7 days. Posts can include CTAs (Call, Book, Learn More) that drive direct conversions.

---

## Post Format Rules

| Field | Rules |
|---|---|
| Image | 1200×900 px JPG/PNG, ≤5MB. NO watermarks. Use real clinic / before-after / team photos when possible. |
| Headline | ≤30 chars (longer gets truncated). Include power word (FREE, NEW, ₹). |
| Body | 250-1500 chars (sweet spot 400-700). First 80 chars matter most — they're what shows in preview. |
| CTA Button | Choose ONE: "Book", "Call now", "Learn more", "Sign up", "Buy" |
| CTA URL | Always link to a money page (NOT homepage). Use UTM tags so GA4 tracks GBP traffic. |

**Standard UTM template** (paste at end of every URL):
```
?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=<post-week>-<topic>
```

Example: `https://drramprabhu.com/gynecomastia?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=w1-gyno-cost`

## Posting Workflow

1. **Sunday evening:** Review the week's two posts, finalize images using Canva
2. **Tuesday morning (10AM):** Post #1 → set GBP "Add update", paste body, attach image, set CTA
3. **Friday morning (10AM):** Post #2 → same workflow
4. **End of each week:** Check GA4 → "Acquisition → Traffic acquisition → Source = gbp" to see clicks per post
5. **Quarterly:** Identify top-performing posts and recycle/expand the topic

## Image Tips

- Use **Canva free templates**: search "Google Business Post"
- Brand colours: primary blue (`#1e40af`), accent gold (`#f59e0b`), white background
- Always include the clinic logo in corner
- Real before/after photos always outperform stock — get patient consent
- For text-heavy posts, keep body to 400-500 chars; image conveys the rest

## Compliance Notes

- ⚠️ Never claim "guaranteed results" or "best in city" without qualification
- ⚠️ Always include "individual results may vary"
- ⚠️ Only use patient photos with documented consent
- ⚠️ Never make medical claims that aren't backed by literature
- ⚠️ Don't disparage other surgeons in posts

In Google Analytics 4:
- **Custom dimension:** `utm_campaign` (already auto-tracked)
- **Conversion event:** Form submit, phone click, WhatsApp click on landing page
- **Report:** Acquisition → Traffic Acquisition → Filter `utm_source = gbp`

You'll see exactly which post drove which booking — over 90 days, you'll know your top 3 themes to repeat.
