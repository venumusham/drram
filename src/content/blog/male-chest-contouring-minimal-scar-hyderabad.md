Male chest contouring is used to create a flatter, more masculine chest shape in patients with chest fullness, gland enlargement, or stubborn fat.

This Short highlights male chest contouring at Dr. Ramprabhu Plastic Surgery Clinic with attention to minimal scars.

## What male chest contouring addresses

Male chest fullness may come from fat, gland tissue, loose skin, or a combination. The treatment plan may include liposuction, gland excision, skin tightening, or a tailored combination.

## Why minimal scar planning matters

Incision placement, gland removal technique, contour blending, and compression after surgery all influence the final appearance.

## Key takeaway

Male chest contouring should be planned after examination. The best results come from treating both gland and fat while respecting natural chest shape.
