Breast lift surgery, also called mastopexy, is designed to improve sagging breasts by removing excess skin and lifting the nipple position.

This Telugu Short by Dr. M. Ram Prabhu explains the basic purpose of breast lift surgery.

## Why breasts sag

Sagging can happen after pregnancy, breastfeeding, weight changes, ageing, or loss of skin elasticity. The breast may lose upper fullness and the nipple may point downward.

## What a breast lift does

A lift reshapes the breast envelope, removes loose skin, and moves the nipple-areola complex to a more suitable position. If volume is also low, augmentation may sometimes be discussed separately.

## Key takeaway

Breast lift surgery is mainly about shape and position, not just size. Consultation helps decide whether lift alone or lift with volume enhancement is suitable.
