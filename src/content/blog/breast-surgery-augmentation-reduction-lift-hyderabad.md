Breast surgery is not one single operation. In plastic surgery, breast procedures are usually planned around what the patient wants to change: size, heaviness, shape, sagging, proportion, comfort, or confidence.

In this video, Dr. M. Ram Prabhu explains three major categories of breast surgery:

- Breast augmentation, for increasing breast size.
- Breast reduction, for reducing heavy breasts.
- Breast lift, for correcting sagging and improving breast position.

Each procedure has a different purpose, technique and recovery plan, so the right choice starts with a personal consultation and examination.

## What are the main types of breast surgery?

Breast surgery can broadly be divided into three groups:

1. **Breast augmentation:** This is done when breast size is smaller than desired and the patient wants a fuller, proportionate shape.
2. **Breast reduction:** This is done when breasts are very heavy and cause physical discomfort such as upper back pain, shoulder discomfort, skin irritation or fungal infection under the breast fold.
3. **Breast lift:** This is done when breasts have become loose or sagging, commonly after pregnancy, breastfeeding, ageing or weight changes.

These procedures may sometimes be combined, depending on the patient’s anatomy and goals.

## Breast augmentation: increasing breast size

Breast augmentation is useful for patients who naturally have smaller breasts or who have lost volume over time. In many patients, small breast size is genetic. In some cases, hormonal or developmental factors may also contribute.

The common surgical method uses a silicone implant. A small incision is usually made in the breast crease, and the implant is placed under the breast tissue or under the chest muscle depending on the patient’s body type, tissue thickness and desired result.

Silicone implants are designed to be medically safe and inert, meaning they do not usually react with the body. The aim is not just to increase size, but to create a natural-looking proportion that suits the patient’s frame.

## Is breast augmentation a day-care surgery?

In many suitable patients, breast augmentation can be performed as a day-care procedure. The surgery commonly takes around two to three hours, and the patient may be able to go home the same day after observation.

Recovery instructions are important. Patients are usually advised about support garments, arm movement, sleeping position, wound care and when to return to normal activities.

## Breast reduction: reducing heavy breasts

Breast reduction is planned for patients with disproportionately heavy breasts. This is not only a cosmetic concern. Heavy breasts can cause:

- Upper back and neck pain.
- Shoulder grooving from bra straps.
- Fungal infection or moisture problems under the breast fold.
- Difficulty exercising comfortably.
- Clothing fit issues and self-consciousness.

In breast reduction surgery, extra breast tissue and skin are removed, the breast is reshaped, and the nipple-areola position is adjusted when needed. The goal is to reduce weight, improve comfort and create a proportionate breast shape.

## Will breast surgery affect milk production?

This is one of the most common questions patients ask.

Breast augmentation generally does not interfere with future milk production because the implant is placed under the breast tissue or chest muscle, and the milk-producing tissue is not removed.

Breast reduction is different because some breast tissue is removed. Theoretically, milk production may reduce to some extent after reduction surgery, but complete absence of milk production is not expected in every patient. The exact risk depends on the surgical technique, amount of tissue removed and individual anatomy.

Patients who are planning pregnancy or breastfeeding in the near future should discuss this clearly during consultation.

## Breast lift: correcting sagging breasts

Breast lift surgery, also called mastopexy, is used when the breast has sagged, the skin has become loose, the nipple position has moved downward, or volume has reduced after pregnancy, breastfeeding, ageing or weight loss.

In a breast lift, excess skin is removed and the breast envelope is tightened. The nipple and areola may be lifted to a more youthful position. The aim is to improve shape and firmness.

If a patient also has significant volume loss, a breast lift may sometimes be combined with an implant or fat grafting, depending on the case.

## How to know which breast procedure is right for you

The right procedure depends on:

- Current breast size and volume.
- Degree of sagging.
- Skin quality.
- Nipple position.
- Chest wall and body proportions.
- Pregnancy and breastfeeding plans.
- Desired cup size and expected shape.

A consultation helps the surgeon examine these factors and explain realistic outcomes, scars, recovery time and long-term expectations.

## Key takeaway

Breast augmentation, breast reduction and breast lift are three different procedures with different goals. Augmentation increases volume, reduction decreases heaviness, and lift corrects sagging.

The best result comes from choosing the procedure that matches the patient’s body, symptoms and goals rather than choosing surgery by name alone.

## FAQs

### Which breast surgery increases breast size?

Breast augmentation increases breast size, usually with a silicone implant placed under the breast tissue or chest muscle.

### Which surgery helps with heavy breasts and back pain?

Breast reduction can reduce breast weight and may help patients who have upper back pain, shoulder discomfort, moisture problems, or fungal infection under the breast fold due to heavy breasts.

### What is the difference between breast lift and breast augmentation?

Breast augmentation adds volume. Breast lift corrects sagging and improves nipple position. Some patients may need both if they have sagging with volume loss.

### Can breast augmentation affect breastfeeding?

Breast augmentation generally does not affect future milk production, but patients planning pregnancy should still discuss this during consultation.

### Can breast reduction affect breastfeeding?

Breast reduction removes some breast tissue, so milk production can theoretically reduce. The risk depends on the technique and how much tissue is removed.

### Is breast surgery a same-day procedure?

In suitable patients, breast augmentation and breast lift can often be done as day-care procedures. Final planning depends on patient health, procedure type and surgical assessment.
