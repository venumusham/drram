Breast augmentation is planned to improve breast volume and proportion while keeping the result natural for the patient's body frame. Safety, implant choice, pocket planning, and realistic expectations matter as much as size.

In this Short, Dr. M. Ram Prabhu discusses breast augmentation with a focus on natural results and patient safety.

## What natural results mean

Natural-looking augmentation is not simply about choosing a smaller or larger implant. The result depends on chest width, tissue thickness, breast shape, skin stretch, implant profile, and placement.

The goal is to create proportion, not an artificial look that does not suit the patient.

## Why consultation matters

During consultation, the surgeon evaluates anatomy, discusses implant options, explains incision placement, and sets realistic expectations about recovery, scars, and long-term care.

## Key takeaway

Breast augmentation should be personalised. A careful surgical plan helps improve volume while keeping the result balanced, safe, and natural-looking.
