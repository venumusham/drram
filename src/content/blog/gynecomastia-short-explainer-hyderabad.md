Gynecomastia is male breast enlargement caused by gland tissue, fat, or both. It can affect confidence, clothing choices, posture, sports, swimming, and social comfort.

This Short is a quick explainer on gynecomastia by Dr. M. Ram Prabhu.

## What patients notice

Patients may notice puffy nipples, a firm disc behind the nipple, rounded chest shape, asymmetry, or fullness that does not improve despite weight loss and exercise.

## Treatment approach

Treatment depends on whether the fullness is mainly fat, gland, skin excess, or a combination. Surgery often combines liposuction and gland excision.

## Key takeaway

If chest enlargement is affecting confidence or does not respond to exercise, a plastic surgery consultation can identify the cause and explain treatment options.
