Breast augmentation surgery increases breast volume and improves proportion using implants or, in selected patients, fat grafting.

This Telugu Short by Dr. M. Ram Prabhu explains breast augmentation surgery for patients considering size enhancement.

## Why patients choose augmentation

Patients may choose augmentation when breast size is naturally small, volume has reduced after pregnancy or weight loss, or body proportion feels imbalanced.

## What planning includes

Planning includes implant size, profile, placement, incision location, tissue thickness, recovery, and realistic expectations about shape.

## Key takeaway

Breast augmentation should be customised. The safest and most natural result comes from matching the surgical plan to the patient's anatomy and goals.
