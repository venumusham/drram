Before planning a hair transplant, the scalp and remaining hair need medical optimisation. A transplant should not be rushed before understanding the cause, stability, and pattern of hair loss.

In this Short, Dr. M. Ram Prabhu discusses prerequisite medical therapy before hair transplant surgery.

## Why medical therapy matters before hair transplant

Hair loss is progressive in many patients. If the existing hair is not stabilised, a transplant may improve one area while untreated hair loss continues elsewhere.

Medical therapy can help improve scalp health, support the hair cycle, and protect existing hair where appropriate.

## Why timing matters

Hair growth follows a cycle. Nutrition, deficiencies, medications, and scalp health can influence the visible result. Optimising these factors before surgery helps create a stronger foundation.

## Key takeaway

A hair transplant is a surgical plan, not just a one-day procedure. Proper medical preparation helps decide whether you are ready for surgery and how to protect long-term results.
