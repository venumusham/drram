Breast reduction surgery helps patients with heavy breasts that cause physical discomfort, posture issues, skin irritation, or difficulty with daily activities.

This Telugu Short by Dr. M. Ram Prabhu introduces breast reduction surgery and why it is both a functional and aesthetic procedure.

## Who may consider breast reduction?

Patients may consider reduction if they have upper back or neck pain, shoulder grooving, sweating or fungal infection under the breast fold, difficulty exercising, or discomfort due to breast heaviness.

## What surgery does

Breast reduction removes excess breast tissue and skin, reshapes the breast, and repositions the nipple-areola complex when needed.

## Key takeaway

Breast reduction is not only cosmetic. For many patients, it improves comfort, movement, posture, and confidence.
