This Short from 22 July 2025 is a clinic awareness post from Dr. Ramprabhu Plastic Surgery Clinic.

The clinic focuses on plastic, cosmetic, reconstructive, hand, wound, and soft tissue procedures for patients in Hyderabad.

## Common reasons patients consult

Patients commonly visit for gynecomastia, liposuction, rhinoplasty, breast procedures, cyst or lipoma removal, scars, wounds, diabetic foot care, hand injuries, and reconstructive surgery.

## Why consultation comes first

A safe plan starts with diagnosis, physical examination, discussion of options, and realistic expectations about recovery and results.

## Key takeaway

If you are unsure which treatment is right, consultation helps clarify the problem and the most suitable next step.
